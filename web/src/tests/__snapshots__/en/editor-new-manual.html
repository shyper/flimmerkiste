Technical

Credits

During the credits the picture shrinks to the top left, next to it you see what's up next, and below highlights of the other channels take turns. If a scene follows the credits, the picture stays big and a small card takes turns at the bottom left: what's up next, then movies on other channels. Without matching credits (at least 30 seconds, at most 3 minutes before the end) it appears in the last minute. The sound stays unchanged. Needs Plex's credits detection (Plex Pass).

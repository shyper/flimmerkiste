Live TV and tuner

How Flimmerkiste appears as a TV tuner in Plex.

Set up in Plex
  1. Open Plex Web → Settings → Live TV & DVR → Set up DVR.
  2. If no device is found, click “Enter network address manually” and enter this address:
    http://192.168.1.10:8089
  3. Plex shows the channels – just confirm with Continue .
  4. For the guide, choose “Use XMLTV guide” and enter this address:
    http://192.168.1.10:8089/epg.xml
  5. Done! The channels appear in Plex under Live TV, including logos and guide.

Tip: when you add new channels, use Live TV & DVR → Scan channels to update the list. Plex updates the programme on its own.
